Lesson 1 of 5

Warp divergence

All lanes of a warp share one instruction stream. At an if where lanes disagree, the warp runs the then path with the other lanes switched off (masked), then the else path with the first group masked. Both paths cost time for the whole warp.

Warp timeExam favourite
time = sum of the lengths of every path at least one lane takes
Paths no lane takes are skipped.
EfficiencyExam favourite
efficiency = (sum over lanes of their path length) ÷ (32 × warp time)
8 lanes on a 10 tick path and 24 on a 30 tick path: 800 ÷ 1280 = 62.5 percent.
Worst case
32 different paths: 32 × L, efficiency 1/32 = 3.1 percent
A 32 times slowdown.
Remember
Divergence only happens inside a warp. If whole warps take the same side, as with if ((tid / 32) % 2 == 0), there is no divergence cost at all.

Quiz 1 question 9

Rakesh's worked solution
  1. One warp of 32 threads, tid = 0 to 31.
  2. Branch A is taken when tid % 4 == 1: tid = 1, 5, 9, 13, 17, 21, 25, 29 (8 lanes).
  3. This is an arithmetic series: 8 terms, first 1, last 29.
  4. Sum = 8 × (1 + 29) ÷ 2 = 8 × 15.
  5. 120

Going deeper: predication

For very short branches the compiler often uses predicated instructions: every lane computes both results and keeps the one its predicate selects. No jump happens, so the cost is just the extra instructions.

Lesson 3 of 5

Warp schedulers

Each SM sub partition has a warp scheduler that issues one instruction per tick from a warp that is ready. Which ready warp it picks changes how well waiting is hidden.

PolicyRuleStrengthWeakness
Round robin (RR)take turns in orderfairall warps reach their loads together and wait together
Greedy then oldest (GTO)keep issuing the same warp until it stalls, then the oldest ready onespreads loads out, good cache localityless fair, a warp can starve
Two levelRR inside a small active group, switch group when all stallbetween the twoneeds tuning of the group size
Rules (workbook 7.4)
one instruction per tick, a load at tick t lets the warp go again at t + L
finish = tick of the last load + L.
Best possible finishExam favourite
finish ≥ (total instructions − 1) + L
4 warps × 12 instructions, L = 12: 47 + 12 = 59. GTO reaches it.
Lesson 4 of 5

How many warps hide the wait?

If each warp does c ticks of work and then waits W ticks, other warps must fill the gap. While one warp waits, the others each need c ticks of their own work.

Warps to hide a waitExam favourite
warps = ⌈(W + c) ÷ c⌉
Work 1 tick, wait 3: 4 warps. One warp leaves 75 percent idle, two leave 50 percent.
Remember
More independent instructions per warp (unrolling, ILP) cut the warps needed: c from 10 to 20 at W = 400 saves 20 warps.
Lesson 5 of 5

From intuition to research

One idea, four depths

A group of 32 must walk together. If some want to go left and some right, the group visits both places one after the other.

Warp time = sum of used path lengths. Efficiency = lane ticks ÷ 32 × time. Q9: ids 1, 5, …, 29 sum to 120. GTO beats RR when loads dominate.

Independent thread scheduling (Volta and later) keeps a program counter per thread, so diverged lanes can interleave, but they still issue separately. Reconverge with __syncwarp() before warp level operations.

Research schedulers (cache conscious wavefront scheduling, CAWS) choose warps to protect L1 locality. Dynamic warp formation regroups lanes from different warps that take the same path.
