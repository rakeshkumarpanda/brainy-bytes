👋Module 1: Meet Claude and get better results

🧒 Beginner friendly

What Claude is. Claude is a large language model. It has read a huge amount of text and writes answers by predicting what text should come next. Two facts follow:

  • It has a knowledge cutoff. It does not know about events after its training unless you give it the facts or turn on web search.
  • It can be wrong and sound sure. Check names, numbers, dates and quotes before you use them.

Your first conversation. Type a request, attach files if they help, and read the answer. If the answer is close but not right, reply with feedback such as "shorter, and add the price". Claude revises the draft instead of starting from zero.

Getting better results. The biggest single tip is to be specific: who it is for, what to include, how long, what tone, and what the result should look like.

The same request, vague and specific · 💬 Prompt to try
Vague:     Write something about our new product.

Specific:  You are writing for busy shop owners in India who sell on WhatsApp.
           Write a 120 word announcement for our new billing app, "QuickBill".
           Mention three things: GST invoices in one tap, works offline, Rs 199 a month.
           Tone: friendly and plain. End with one line telling them how to try it free for 14 days.
What happens

The vague prompt gets a generic paragraph. The specific one says who it is for, what to include, how long, what tone and how to end, so the first draft is close to usable.

Add thisExample
Who it is for"for busy shop owners who sell on WhatsApp"
The task"write a 120 word announcement"
Context and facts"three features: GST invoices, offline, Rs 199 a month"
Format and tone"friendly and plain, end with one call to action"
An example, if you have one"here is last month's announcement we liked"

🖥️Module 1: Chat, Cowork and Code on your desktop

🧒 Beginner friendly

The Claude Desktop app has three ways to work. They share one home screen and message box.

ModeBest forExample
Chatback and forth questions, drafts, ideas"Rewrite this email to sound warmer."
Coworklonger tasks with many steps, on your files"Sort the 40 PDFs in my Invoices folder by month and make a summary sheet."
Codesoftware work: reading, changing and testing code"Fix the failing login test."

Cowork uses the same agent design as Claude Code, without a terminal. You describe the task, review Claude's plan, then let it run. Facts worth knowing:

  • It needs a paid plan: Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise.
  • Claude can open local files only in folders you connect, and it must ask you before deleting any file for good.
  • It has permission modes: Manual asks before actions, Auto checks each action for safety first, and Skip runs without checks. Use Skip only for tasks you fully trust.
  • Cowork uses more of your plan's usage than chat. Use chat for simple tasks and check Settings, then Usage.
🏫 Real scenario: a school officeEvery term the office gets 300 fee receipts as PDFs. In Chat, the clerk asks how to name files. In Cowork, she connects the Receipts folder and asks Claude to rename every file as class, roll number and date, then list any receipt with a missing amount. She reviews the plan, presses go, and checks the list at the end.

Projects, the facts to remember

  • Every plan can use projects. Free accounts can make up to five.
  • Instructions say how Claude should behave in every chat of the project. Knowledge holds the files it can look things up in.
  • On paid plans, when the knowledge grows near the context limit, Claude switches to RAG by itself and can hold up to 10 times more content. It then searches the files and reads only the relevant parts.
  • On Team and Enterprise plans you can share a project with Can view or Can edit access.
Project instructions for your own study project · 📄 Ready to copy
# Project instructions: "Claude 101 study buddy"

You are my study partner for the Claude 101 course and the Claude Certified Associate exam.

- Explain each idea in plain words first, then give one real work example.
- When I say "quiz me", ask 5 multiple choice questions, one at a time.
  Wait for my answer before you show whether it was right and why.
- If you are not sure a fact is current, say so and tell me which Help Center page to check.
- Keep answers under 200 words unless I ask for more.

Artifacts, the facts to remember

  • Claude makes an artifact when the content is substantial and stands on its own, usually more than 15 lines, and you are likely to edit or reuse it.
  • Your artifacts are saved in the Artifacts tab of the sidebar. They start private to you until you share them.
  • Docs export to Word, PDF, Markdown or Google Docs. Decks export to PowerPoint, PDF or Google Slides.
  • An artifact can call Claude itself and work like a small app. No API key is needed, and each person who uses it spends their own plan's usage.

Skills, the facts to remember

  • Anthropic's built in skills for Excel, Word, PowerPoint and PDF files are there for every user, and Claude uses them by itself.
  • Skills need code execution turned on: Settings, then Capabilities, then Code execution and file creation.
  • Upload your own skill as a ZIP under Customize, then Skills. Chapter 7 has a screenshot for each step.

🔌Module 3: Connectors, enterprise search and Research

🧒 Beginner friendly

Connectors let Claude read from and act in the apps you already use, such as Google Drive, Gmail, Slack or your own tools. They are built on MCP, the same standard Chapter 10 teaches.

  • Claude gets your permissions. If you cannot open a file in the source app, Claude cannot either.
  • Turn connectors on for a chat with the + button, then Connectors. Add new ones under Customize, then Connectors.
  • On Team and Enterprise plans an owner enables each connector first, and can set each tool to Always allow, Needs approval or Blocked.

Enterprise search (Team and Enterprise) is a ready made project, shown as "Ask" plus your organisation's name, that searches across company tools in one place. An owner sets it up once. Results respect each person's permissions, and Anthropic does not index the data from those tools to answer queries.

Research (paid plans) is for questions that need many sources. Claude runs several searches that build on each other, reads them, and writes an answer. Turn on web search, then click + and choose Research.

A good Research request · 💬 Prompt to try
Research how small clinics in India are using AI for appointment reminders.
I need: 5 real examples with sources, what each one costs if stated,
and the main privacy rules they had to follow. Put the sources at the end.
What happens

With Research on, Claude runs several searches that build on each other, reads the pages, and writes a cited answer. It takes longer than a normal reply and uses more of your plan.

You need...Use
facts from your own Drive or emaila connector
one answer from all company tools at onceenterprise search
a deep, cited answer from the webResearch

📝The Claude Certified Associate exam (CCAO-F)

If you want a credential with a pass mark, Anthropic has a proctored exam called Claude Certified Associate, Foundations, code CCAO-F. It is for people who use Claude as a work tool, such as operations, marketing, project management, teaching and communications. It is not aimed at developers who build on the API.

ItemDetail from the exam guide (version 1.0, July 2026)
Questions60, multiple choice and multiple response. Each question says how many answers to pick.
Time120 minutes
Pass marka scaled score of 720 on a scale of 100 to 1,000
FeeUSD 99
Whereproctored through Pearson VUE, online or at a test centre
Valid for12 months. On time renewal is a free assessment without a proctor.
Retakeswait 14 days after the first fail, 30 after the second, 90 after the third. At most four tries in 12 months.

The seven domains and their weight in the exam. Watch the bars: the biggest one is about checking Claude's answers, not about writing prompts.

Output evaluation and validation21%
Workflow integration and solution design16%
Governance, risk and responsible use15%
Prompting and task execution14%
Product and model selection12%
Configuration and knowledge management12%
Troubleshooting and optimisation10%
Third party guides say registration is open only to people at organisations in the Claude Partner Network, using a work email from that organisation. Rules can change, so read the certifications page on the Anthropic Partner Academy before you plan your date.

Part 11

🧩Exam style scenarios, worked through

🧒 Beginner friendly

The exam asks what you would do in a work situation. Here is how to think through three typical ones.

📜 Scenario 1: a confident citationClaude summarises a new tax rule and names "Section 12(4)(b)". You are about to send it to your manager. Best move: open the official text and check that section says what Claude claims, before you send. Why: models can produce details that look exact but are wrong. This is Domain 2.
📨 Scenario 2: 2,000 short replies a dayA support team needs short, simple reply drafts, fast and cheap. Best move: a smaller, faster model such as Haiku, with a clear template. Why: the task is simple and high volume, so speed and cost matter more than deep reasoning. This is Domain 3.
🔒 Scenario 3: a sheet with customer accountsYou want trends from a spreadsheet with customer names and account numbers, and policy forbids uploading that kind of personal data. Best move: remove or mask the names and account numbers first, then upload. Why: Claude needs the patterns, not the identities. This is Domain 6.
For every scenario ask yourself three things in order: is it safe and allowed, is the answer checked, and is this the simplest tool that does the job.
